[docs/brand/CLAUDE_BRANDING.md](docs/brand/CLAUDE_BRANDING.md). It records the
owner's selected 0xSnow designs and distinguishes supplied brand assets from
what the app currently renders.

  are frozen; use the packaged files rather than redrawing or retyping them.
- [docs/brand/0xsnow/selection.json](docs/brand/0xsnow/selection.json) records the
  exact selection. The new brand package is in `docs/brand/0xsnow/`.
- [docs/BRAND.md](docs/BRAND.md) describes the current product styling, including
  retained chairlift references. [docs/HANDOFF.md](docs/HANDOFF.md) describes
  application behavior and infrastructure.

  does not itself replace header, email, favicon or installed-app artwork.

Keep snowfall charts blue/ice, source times and units visible, existing chart
geometry and navigation behavior intact, and the light/dark preferences stable.
