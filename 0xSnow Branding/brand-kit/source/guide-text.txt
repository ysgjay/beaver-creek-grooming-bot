THE CORE IDEA
A gauge, a zero, a snowline.
Two inward ticks suggest measurement. The blue drift completes the lower contour of
the zero itself. Its open center keeps the symbol simple at a glance.
Keep the outer bottom edge of the zero aligned with the wordmark
baseline. Match the top to the capital S; preserve the selected width and
the two left-hand ticks.
The blue base is part of the silhouette. Do not add a dark rim beneath it.
