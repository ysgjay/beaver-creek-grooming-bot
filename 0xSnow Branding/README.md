# 0xSnow Branding

One place to find every 0xSnow brand and logo file.

The files the app serves stay where the app loads them (`public/` and `public/brand/`), so moving them would break the site. This folder links to them and holds the working files that the app does not serve: mockups and the archived Chair 0 logos. When you add new logo or brand work, put the working files here and the served exports in `public/brand/`.

## Brand guide

- [docs/BRAND.md](../docs/BRAND.md): palette, fonts, logo files, logo anatomy, burgundy rules, light and dark surfaces.
